SKY + ULF · INDEPENDENT, OPEN SOURCE

Open tools that run on your own ground.

Skyulf is an independent, open-source family of products. Kayra makes machine learning you can verify. Koloni puts a colony of agents to work together. Both run where your data already lives.

Sky + Ulf

Sky is the open space above Earth, where the sun, moon, stars, and clouds live. Ulf means “wolf,” with Nordic roots, and the wolf is also a strong symbol in Turkic tradition. Together it fits the project: independent and community-driven.
