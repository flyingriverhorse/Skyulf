Questions about control and deployment.

Does customer data have to leave its infrastructure?

No. Skyulf is designed for local and self-hosted use. The hosted demo is for evaluation.

Can a team use only the Python engine?

Yes. skyulf-core installs independently from PyPI under Apache-2.0.

Can a small team start without Redis and PostgreSQL?

Yes. Skyulf can begin with SQLite, local storage, and background threads, then add the heavier infrastructure later.
