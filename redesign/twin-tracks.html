Questions before trying it

Clear answers.

Do I have to choose between the platform and Python?

No. They are two entrances to the same product philosophy. Use the visual workspace when it helps, use skyulf-core directly when code is the better interface, and export notebooks when you need a portable artifact.

Where does my data go?

Skyulf is designed to run locally or on infrastructure you control. The hosted demo is for evaluation; real deployments can remain self-hosted.

What happens after training?

Runs can be compared, models can be versioned in the registry, promoted to inference endpoints, and monitored for drift.
