OPENING STATEMENT

For the record, read aloud.

Ladies and gentlemen of the jury: somewhere right now, a model is being celebrated. Ninety-four percent accuracy. Confetti. A deployment ticket is printed.

But that score is a fraud. It was computed on data the model had already seen. When the same model is judged fairly — on data it never touched during fitting — it scores seventy-one percent.

The People will prove that Skyulf is the instrument that catches this crime in the act: a visual pipeline builder where every model carries a fingerprint, and the validation cell is sealed by law.

COUNT I — THE CRIME SCENE

The seven stages where the offense is committed — or prevented.

The People submit that a machine learning pipeline has seven stages, and the crime may be committed at any one of them.

  • 1 · Datasources. Data is registered and versioned on entry. Exhibit A.
  • 2 · EDA. Exploratory analysis — distributions, missing values, correlations — before a single fit. Exhibit B.
  • 3 · Build. Pipelines are drawn on a visual canvas from 35 node types. Nodes split into learn and apply, so the court can always tell what was fitted and what was merely scored. Exhibit C.
  • 4 · Train. Jobs run under supervision, with full audit log. Exhibit D.
  • 5 · Experiments. Tuning by Optuna, grid, random, or halving search; ensembles; SHAP explanations; notebook export. Exhibit E.
  • 6 · Registry. Every model is fingerprinted and issued a model card before it may travel. Exhibit F.
  • 7 · Deploy & Drift. Serving, then continuous monitoring by KS, PSI, Wasserstein, and KL divergence. Exhibits G–H.

Exhibit II — Honest Validation
71%
Skyulf's validate_leakage_safety() re-scores on untouched rows. Admitted into evidence.

The People call their expert witness to explain the mechanism.

A — Expert Witness (Senior Pipeline Engineer):

Leakage is what happens when information from outside the training period crosses into evaluation. The cure is architectural. In Skyulf, nodes are divided: learn nodes are allowed to fit, apply nodes are not. Validation data is fingerprinted and sealed before training begins, and validate_leakage_safety() is the bailiff at the door. If the barrier was crossed, the run does not leave the building.
