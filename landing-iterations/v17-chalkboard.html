Lesson 02 — the weekly routine

Seven steps, every project

1.

Connect your data

Postgres, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, CSV — write it on the board once, reuse it everywhere.

2.

Explore first

EDA dashboards show distributions, correlations and missing values before you fit anything.

3.

Design on the canvas

Learn nodes study the training set. Apply nodes face the world blind. Underline that — it's on the exam.

4.

Train & tune

Optuna, grid, random, halving — every trial written down where you can find it.

5.

Register the model

Fingerprint plus model card. No more "which data fitted this scaler?" mysteries.

6.

Deploy

REST endpoints or batch jobs. Same artifact you tested — not a re-implementation.

7.

Watch for drift

KS, PSI, Wasserstein, KL — the hall monitors of your features.

Lesson 04 — questions from the back row

FAQ

Is Skyulf free?

Yes — core is Apache-2.0, platform is AGPLv3. git clone, docker compose up, and you're teaching your own class.

Is it enterprise-ready?

It's honest v0.1: real code, 2,266 passing tests, real docs. No inflated claims — we grade ourselves.

Why not just use a notebook?

Notebooks are lovely for homework. In production, someone asks "which data fitted this scaler?" — Skyulf's registry answers by construction.

What powers it?

A scikit-learn engine, Python 3.12, pandas or Polars. SHAP for explanations, Optuna for tuning, notebook export for the road.
