Skyulf landing page — 22 design iterations

Each iteration is self-contained (no build step, no Tailwind CDN) and uses the real product screenshots from static/img. The original root index.html is untouched.

V1

Editorial Serif

Magazine-style longform page. Fraunces serif on warm paper, numbered sections, a clickable lifecycle index, and the leakage story told as an essay with a verdict panel.

Fraunces + Inter · light paper #faf7f2 · teal accent
 V2

Glass Aurora

Dark startup aesthetic with animated aurora glows, grain overlay and glass bento grids. Lifecycle shown as a sticky rail with switchable panels.

Space Grotesk · dark #05060f · gradient glass
 V3

Brutalist Terminal

Loud and unapologetic. Hard 3px borders, marquee ticker, terminal hero with blinking cursor, hard-shadow buttons, and a red/green 94% vs 71% score showdown.

Archivo + IBM Plex Mono · cream/black · yellow highlight
 V4

Scrollytelling

A narrative page: scroll progress bar, staggered hero, and a sticky chapter column that drives a pinned stack of real screenshots through the 7 pipeline stages.

Sora · dark #0c0f14 · IntersectionObserver frames
 V5

Swiss Grid Light

Pure white spec-sheet. Hairline rules, a single electric-blue accent, mono key-value facts, lifecycle as table rows, leakage rendered as a comparison datasheet. Zero JavaScript.

Instrument Sans + IBM Plex Mono · white · #2545ff accent
 V6

Mission Control

Ops-room energy. Dark telemetry theme with a live animated pipeline graph (flowing SVG nodes), stat tiles, a lifecycle "board," and the leakage story rendered as a scan terminal with PASS/FAIL output.

Manrope + JetBrains Mono · dark #0a0e14 · teal telemetry
 V7

Blueprint

An engineering drawing sheet. Deep blueprint blue with a fine grid, title block, dimension lines, dashed connectors, numbered lifecycle stages, and REJECTED/APPROVED revision stamps for the leaked vs. honest score.

Barlow Condensed + IBM Plex Mono · blueprint blue · cyan line
 V8

Soft Pastel

Warm, friendly, approachable. Cream background with organic blob shapes, pastel mint/peach/lavender cards, a horizontal snap-scroll lifecycle rail, and rounded "cozy" stat tiles. Designed to feel safe, not intimidating.

DM Sans · cream #fbf5ee · pastel multi-accent
 V9

Synthwave

Retro-arcade neon. Sunset grid floor, glowing pink/cyan type, CRT scanline overlay, and the leakage duel staged as a neon "VS" boss battle. High-energy, high-contrast, unapologetically loud.

Orbitron + Chakra Petch · #0d0221 · neon pink/cyan
 V10

Type Manifesto

Typography as the whole design. Massive outlined Anton headlines, thick black rules, a scrolling statement strip, and the leaked-vs-honest score as two giant numerals. Images are secondary; words do the work.

Anton + Inter · off-white · red-orange accent
 V11

Notebook

A Jupyter notebook as the page. In[N]/Out[N] cell gutters, syntax-highlighted pipeline code, each lifecycle stage a code cell that outputs a real screenshot figure, and the leakage story as a red LeakageError traceback with a corrected output cell.

Inter + JetBrains Mono · #f4f5f7 · Jupyter orange
 V12

Photocopied punk-flyer energy. Fluoro pink and riso blue inks with deliberate misregistration shadows, tape strips, rotated cut-out cards, a starburst badge, and the leaked 94% struck through in wavy ink.

Syne + Cutive Mono · paper #f5efe3 · pink/blue inks

A floating machine-city. Isometric tile map of the seven pipeline stages bobbing in space, gradient-bordered glass cards, and the leakage duel as two hovering platforms — one glitching, one stable.

Outfit + JetBrains Mono · indigo #16142e · floating tiles

Court Record

New structure, not just a new skin: the whole page is the trial transcript of The People v. Data Leakage. Docket header, numbered transcript lines, screenshots taped in as Exhibits A–H, the 94% cross-examined and struck from the record, and a sealed Order of the Court instead of a marketing footer.

Special Elite + Libre Baskerville · aged paper · red seal stamps
