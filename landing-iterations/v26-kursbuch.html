Stations of the line

Every lifecycle stage is a station with a real platform: a product module you can stand on. Eight stops, photographed as they actually look.

01

Ingest

ING · Halt 01

The load comes aboard here. Skyulf connects your data to the network without ceremony — files on disk or objects in the cloud, same platform, same timetable.

  • CSV, Excel, JSON and Parquet sources
  • S3-compatible storage, or your local disk
02

EDA

EDA · Halt 02

Automated exploration while the data is still warm from the load. Skyulf profiles it end to end so you board the pipeline already knowing the terrain.

  • Statistical profiling, distributions, correlations, outliers
  • Segmentation, causal discovery, target analysis
03

Pipeline

PIP · Halt 03

Node-based pipelines, wired by hand: preprocessing, feature engineering, splitting, modeling. The canvas is a first-class interface — and everything on it can equally be written as code with skyulf-core.

  • Preprocessing, feature engineering, splitting, modeling nodes
  • Visual workflows export to Jupyter notebooks — full or compact
04

Train

TRN · Halt 04

Training departs as background work — Celery + Redis queues, or background threads where that fits. Jobs run on time; you are not chained to the platform while they move.

  • Background training via Celery + Redis
  • Background threads as the lighter alternative
05

Compare

CMP · Halt 05

Experiments pull in side by side and are compared on evidence. No folklore about which model is best — the comparison is the answer.

  • Experiment comparison across runs
  • Decisions made on recorded results, not memory
06

Registry

REG · Halt 06

The depot. Models are registered and versioned with their history on record, ready for the next departure — and the audit history keeps the books honest.

  • Model registry with versions
  • Audit history on record
07

Deploy

DEP · Halt 07

Departure into production. A pipeline is a scheduled service once deployed — it serves inference and holds its place in the timetable.

  • Deployment from the registry
  • Inference served from deployed pipelines
08

Monitor

MON · Halt 08

Track inspection. Deployed models are watched for drift, with reports and diagnostics when the line shifts under load. The service does not end at departure.

  • Drift monitoring for deployed models
  • Drift reports and diagnostics

Operations · Betriebszentrale

What keeps a railway honest happens off the passenger platforms: freight that moves at night, track that gets inspected, a log that records everything.

Night freight · Güterverkehr

Background training

Training jobs ride as night freight on Celery + Redis workers — or background threads on lighter routes. Dispatch a job from the canvas or from code and it moves through the queue while you sleep. Freight doesn't wait for you, and it doesn't need you on the footplate.

Track inspection · Gleiskontrolle

Drift monitoring

Deployed models are inspected like track: Skyulf watches for drift and writes the report when the line has shifted under your data. Diagnostics show where, so the repair is targeted instead of guesswork.

See the inspection at the Monitor station — Gleis 08.
Operations log · Betriebslogbuch

Audit history

Everything that moves on this network is written down. The audit history is the operations log — what ran, what was registered, what departed, what was inspected. When a question comes up at 2 a.m., the log answers it.

Recorded in the operations log
